# Demo walkthrough

Recommended length: 6 to 8 minutes.

1. Start the local application.
2. Process the four-person agency transcript.
3. Show summary, decisions, tasks, deadlines, risks and open questions.
4. Upload the five-person AI automation audio.
5. Show processing and diarized speaker segments.
6. Map speakers to names, roles and emails.
7. Confirm mappings and save voice profiles.
8. Review and edit the generated results.
9. Confirm the final notes.
10. Send the approved follow-up email to a test recipient.
11. Show the email log, meeting history and task dashboard.
12. Upload the eight-speaker in-room audio.
13. Show all detected speakers and manual mapping.
14. Upload the recurring-speaker test audio.
15. Show suggested matches, correct any wrong suggestion and confirm manually.
16. Open People / Voice Profiles and demonstrate the delete control.
17. State the current limitations and next steps.
